# 🎓 School-to-Success

> **An AI-powered learning platform designed to guide students from school to real-world success** 🚀
> Empowering learners through **Coding 💻, Entrepreneurship 💡, and Ethical Values 🌱** with a modern dashboard-driven experience.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview

**School-to-Success** is a Smart India Hackathon (SIH) project aimed at building an engaging, student-centric learning ecosystem. The platform provides:

* A **personalized learning dashboard**
* **AI-assisted study guidance**
* Interactive **quizzes and learning paths**
* Progress tracking, streaks, and achievements

The goal is to bridge the gap between **academic learning** and **real-world skills**, ensuring holistic student development.

---

## 🧠 Problem Statement

Traditional education systems often:

* Focus heavily on theory rather than practical skills
* Lack personalized guidance for students
* Ignore entrepreneurship and ethical development
* Fail to motivate students through progress tracking

🔴 **Result:** Students feel disengaged, directionless, and unprepared for real-world challenges.

---

## ✅ Solution Approach

School-to-Success addresses these challenges by:

* Offering **structured learning paths** in key skill areas
* Using **AI-driven recommendations** for daily study focus
* Gamifying learning through **streaks and achievements**
* Providing a **centralized dashboard** for tracking growth

---

## 🖥️ Features

### 💻 Coding Track

* Learn HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and more
* Interactive quizzes and challenges
* Project-based learning approach

### 💡 Entrepreneurship Track

* Develop problem-solving and leadership skills
* Learn idea validation and pitching
* Encourages creative and innovative thinking

### 🌱 Ethical Behavior Track

* Case studies on honesty, teamwork, and responsibility
* Focus on empathy and moral decision-making
* Real-life value-based scenarios

### 🤖 AI Study Coach

* Personalized daily learning suggestions
* Skill-based improvement recommendations
* Balanced focus on technical and soft skills

### 📊 Student Dashboard

* Profile overview
* Learning progress bar
* Streak tracking 🔥
* Achievements and milestones 🏆

### 📝 Quizzes

* Interactive MCQs
* Instant feedback
* Knowledge reinforcement
